The main purpose of this project is to ensure that a light source remains available when the main electricity supply is interrupted, making it useful for homes, offices, workshops, and other environments where emergency lighting is required. The system is designed with a rechargeable battery, LED lighting circuit, charging section, power detection circuit, and automatic switching mechanism**. Remote Controlled AC Switch is an electronic switching system that I personally designed and developed to control AC-powered electrical appliances remotely. The main objective of this project is to provide a convenient and practical method of switching household electrical loads ON and OFF without directly operating a conventional wall switch. The system combines a remote control interface, receiver circuit, control electronics, and relay-based AC switching section. When a command is received from the remote controller, the control circuit processes the signal and activates or deactivates the relay, allowing the connected AC load to be switched accordingly. The relay provides electrical isolation between the low-voltage control circuit and the AC load section. I personally worked on the circuit design, remote signal receiving section, control logic, relay driver circuit, AC switching stage, component selection, assembly, testing, and troubleshooting. Through this project, I gained practical experience in **remote control systems, digital signal processing, relay control, AC load switching, electronic circuit design, and electrical safety.
